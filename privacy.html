Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data BKS Infralink ("we", "us") collects through this website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

What we collect

  • Details you send us through the contact or quote forms, by email, phone or WhatsApp: your name, company, phone number, email address and anything you write about your project, including links to files.
  • Basic technical data that our hosting provider records when you load a page, such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.
  • A preference stored in your browser remembering whether you chose the Ink or Paper view on the Work page. It never leaves your device.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To carry out and invoice work you ask us to do.
  • To keep records we are required to keep by law, such as tax records.

We rely on your consent, which you give when you send a form, and on our legitimate need to run the business. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing you did not ask for.

Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run this site and reply to you: our website host, our email provider and, if enabled, the form service that delivers form messages to our inbox. These providers may store data outside India under their own security commitments. We share project details with your client or their inspection agency only when the job requires it and you have agreed.

Drawings on this site

The drawings shown in our portfolio are redrawn from our own CAD files. Household names, house numbers, purchase order numbers and other identifying details have been removed. If you believe a drawing still identifies you or your property, write to us and we will take it down.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become work are deleted within 12 months. Project and billing records are kept for as long as tax law requires, currently up to 8 years.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can withdraw consent at any time. Email us at our email address and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiries and project files is limited to the people who need them to do the work.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
